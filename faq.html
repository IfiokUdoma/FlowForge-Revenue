About the Service
What exactly is revenue engineering?+

Revenue engineering is the systematic process of identifying where money exits a business's funnel, quantifying that loss in exact dollars, and fixing it with evidence-backed precision. It's not copywriting. It's a diagnostic and implementation discipline that treats every conversion problem as a financial problem with a measurable dollar value.

Where a copywriter rewrites your homepage to "sound better," a revenue engineer rewrites it because the current version is losing $47K/month at a specific funnel point — and they can show you the math.

Is this just copywriting with a different name?+

No. Copywriting produces words. Revenue engineering produces financial outcomes. A copywriter is briefed and writes. A revenue engineer audits your funnel to find where money exits, quantifies the loss, forms a hypothesis about why it's happening, writes the fix, and tracks the result against a revenue baseline. If the numbers don't move, the work isn't done.

Who is this service for?+

FlowForge Revenue works exclusively with B2B fintech and payment infrastructure companies — payment processors, embedded finance platforms, open banking API providers, cross-border payment products, and BaaS providers.

The right person to engage us is whoever owns revenue growth: Head of Growth, Head of Product, VP of Sales/BD, or the CEO/Founder at earlier-stage companies.

Why only fintech?+

Because focus is how expertise is built. The buyer psychology, compliance objections, infrastructure economics, and enterprise sales dynamics in fintech are domain-specific. We don't do e-commerce, B2B SaaS outside fintech, or consumer products. We do fintech. That specificity is the value.

What's the difference between the free diagnosis and a paid audit?+

The free diagnosis covers one funnel point — your choice. You receive findings and 3–5 fix recommendations. It's a diagnosis document, not a treatment plan. You'll know exactly what's wrong and what it costs you.

A paid full audit covers your entire funnel end-to-end with a complete dollar-quantified leak map, prioritized fix roadmap, exec-ready report, and 30-day implementation guide.

Is the free diagnosis actually free?+

Yes — no credit card, no discovery call required first. You submit the form, we review your funnel, and we deliver written findings within 5 business days. If we review your funnel and find nothing significant, we'll tell you that honestly. We don't manufacture problems that don't exist.

Pricing & Process
How does payment work?+

All paid engagements are structured 50% upfront and 50% on delivery. You never pay the full amount before receiving the work. The upfront payment confirms the engagement. The second payment is due when the deliverable is complete and accepted by you.

Once scope is agreed, we send a payment link directly to your email. For Revenue Partnerships, the structure is performance-based — we earn a percentage of the measurable revenue uplift we generate. No upfront fee.

Why no free trials? The diagnosis feels like one.+

A free trial gives away the full service with no commitment from either side and attracts people who want free work, not clients who want results.

The free diagnosis is a diagnostic tool that delivers real findings you can act on immediately. It's not a sample of the full service — it's the first step in a deliberate process that only continues if there's a real leak worth fixing. Think of it like a medical diagnosis: the doctor doesn't operate for free, but the diagnosis tells you whether surgery is needed at all.

How long does an engagement take?+

Free diagnosis: Written findings delivered within 5 business days.

Full Revenue Audit: Typically 10–14 business days depending on funnel complexity.

Revenue Engineering Sprint: 90-day minimum. First 30 days: diagnosis and fix design. Days 31–90: implementation, tracking, iteration.

Revenue Partnership: 6-month minimum with monthly sprints and continuous tracking.

What do you need from us to get started?+

For the free diagnosis: just your form submission with your company website and the funnel area you want audited. We can work from your public-facing product — no internal access required at this stage.

For paid engagements: access to basic analytics (traffic volume, conversion rates, average deal sizes) helps us quantify leaks accurately. We'll specify exactly what we need at the start.

What if I'm not satisfied with the work?+

Because all paid engagements are 50% upfront and 50% on delivery, you review the deliverable before making the final payment. If it doesn't meet the agreed scope, we address it before the second payment is due.

How do I know the revenue numbers are accurate?+

Every dollar figure in our reports is derived from your actual data — traffic volume, conversion rates at each funnel step, average deal size, and monthly payment volume. We show you the model and the full calculation, not just the output.

In the free diagnosis, where we work from your public product, we use conservative industry benchmarks and state clearly when a figure is a benchmark versus an actual data point. You should always be able to verify any number we produce.
